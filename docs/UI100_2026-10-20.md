# UI100 — 100 things to make the site snappier, calmer and lighter (2026-10-20)

Owner ask (2026-10-20): *"Go through the entire user interface from a PHONE and
from a PC and suggest 100 things worth correcting for a snappier, smoother,
easier on the eye, not 10000 things on the screen. Give me a TLDR summary. Then
we execute."*

How the list was made: every nav page plus the chart page was driven headless at
390×844 (phone) and 1440×900 (PC) on the live published data, screenshotted top
to bottom (rows expanded, the More sheet opened, PhaseMap in each density), and
measured on a 4× CPU-throttled phone profile (requests, bytes, DOM nodes, page
height). The measurements that drove the list:

| page | phone height | DOM nodes | data on load |
|---|---|---|---|
| deck | 8,173 px | 3,610 | 2.3 MB (bot book ×2, bot_rules ×3, both other markets) |
| journal | 17,987 px | 4,496 | 1.1 MB |
| PhaseMap | 60,732 px | 7,214 | 2.2 MB |
| alerts | 5,471 px | 6,635 | 1.0 MB |
| system | 14,999 px | 893 | 3.3 MB (1.8 MB of backtests) |

On a phone the deck pinned ~245 px of header + toolbar + section bar (29% of the
screen) above the list, and the chart started 360 px down.

**Nothing here moves a trade rule, a grade, a gate or the book.** Every item is
display, layout, wording or loading. One item changes a *display default* the
owner should know about: **#27** (the deck opens on Longs, since the book is
long-only; Shorts stay one tap away and the choice is remembered).

**Status key** — **DO** shipped in this batch · **SKIP** not shipped, reason
given · **OWNER** needs the owner's call.
