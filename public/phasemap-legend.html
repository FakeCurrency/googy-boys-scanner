KEY & LEGEND every badge, colour and term on the cards

PhaseMap reads one repeating cycle: a range forms (accumulation), the range's lows or highs get run (manipulation), then the real move leaves (expansion). Everything on a card is a zone — a price band — never a single price.

PHASE BADGES

TRAP SET
Price is coiling in an unusually tight range and equal lows (or highs) are resting nearby — the liquidity pool is loaded. Nothing to do yet.
SWEPT
The resting lows were run and price closed back inside the range. The trap has sprung — now the market has a few sessions to prove it.
DISPLACED
A candle with an oversized range, a tiny opposing wick and a close near its extreme printed — real intent confirmed. Day-of state.
RUNNING
The expansion leg is live. Pullbacks are expected to stay shallow; the target zones above are the draw.
STALLED
Price touched the 50% area of the leg. The continuation thesis is finished — the ticker moves to the rotation watchlist where deep-retracement logic applies.
COMPLETE
Every mapped target zone was consumed. The cycle is finished; the ticker resets to neutral.
DEAD
A daily close through the hard invalidation floor. The swept level did not hold; the setup is void.

ZONE COLOURS

AMBER
Accumulation & the trap: the demand/supply zone (the manipulation wick) and trap-set ranges.
RED
Manipulation & invalidation: the 50% momentum zone (dies on a touch) and the hard structural zone (dies on a close through it).
GREEN
Targets & expansion: the liquidity objectives the move is drawn toward, nearest first (T1, T2…).
TEAL
The shallow continuation band (23.6–38.2% pullback) — reference only, and only in an expansion regime.

GLOSSARY — PLAIN ENGLISH

SweepPrice trades below an obvious low (where stops sit), then closes back above it. The stops get taken; the trap prints as a wick.
DisplacementAn unusually large candle (1.75× normal range) that closes near its extreme with almost no opposing wick. It is how the market proves the sweep was a trap and not a breakdown.
Equal lows / highsTwo or more swing points at almost the same price. Cheap, illiquid stocks often print these messy double taps instead of one clean wick — they count as a sweep when taken and reclaimed.
Anchor openThe first traded price of the year, quarter or month. The yearly open is the heaviest level on the chart; manipulation often happens just beyond an anchor before the real move.
RegimeEXPANSION = a displacement candle printed recently and pullbacks stay shallow (<50%). ROTATION = slow and wicky; deep retracements are normal — different playbook (the Fib scanner's territory).
ConfluenceHow many independent reasons point at the same zone (e.g. a fib extension + old equal highs + the quarterly open = ×3). More sources, more weight.
SMT divergenceA crack in correlation between two related stocks: one sweeps its low while its pair holds. Confirmation bonus only — coming in a later phase.
Zone statesUNTESTED → TESTED (a wick touched it) → RESPECTED (touched and price closed back away) → CONSUMED (a target closed through) or VIOLATED (an invalidation triggered). Zones never come back to life.
