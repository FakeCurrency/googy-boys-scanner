ONE MARKET, THREE LENSES — WHICH SCANNER CATCHES WHICH SHAPE

CASE STUDY — JGH 2026: A 4× RUN PHASEMAP CORRECTLY NEVER CALLED

What happenedJade Gas ground along ~$0.022–0.030 for months, then ran to $0.110 through May–June 2026 — roughly 4× from the accumulation area.
What the engine sawDisplacement-quality candles printed on 14, 20 and 29 May — but the lows never undercut the prior 40-bar low and reclaimed it. No sweep → no trap → no signal. The state machine stayed NEUTRAL the entire run.
The 5-year record on JGH8 bullish PhaseMap signals since 2022: one clean winner (Aug 2022 — T1 in 6 sessions, +21.6% at 20 bars) and five that died on the hard floor. Mechanically, JGH is a stock this playbook has a poor record on.
The lessonThe 2026 bottom was a base-breakout shape (the Specs lens), not a stop-run shape. Missing it wasn't a failure of PhaseMap — it was the wrong lens for that trade. Multiple lenses exist because no single one sees everything.

WHAT THE 5-YEAR REPLAY MEASURED (17,180 SIGNALS ACROSS 3 MARKETS)

Liquidity is the edge, not the enemyASX liquid names: 40.9% reached T1 inside 20 sessions with −8.3% average worst drawdown. Illiquid names: 25.6% and −14.4%. Stocks ≥$1: 38.4% hit. Cents stocks: 26.0%. The methodology scans everything; the measured edge lives at the liquid end. That's what the HIDE ILLIQUID toggle is for.
A+ means different things per marketNASDAQ: A+ (anchor-context) setups hit T1 52.5% vs 41.8% for plain A — the badge earns its keep. ASX: A+ 28.5% vs A 29.6% — no edge from the anchor bonus there so far. Same rules, different market character.
The edge is front-loadedASX signals beat random entry over 5 and 10 sessions (+1.3%/+1.4% vs +0.3%/+0.5%) but NOT over 20 (+1.3% vs +3.1% random — microcap drift catches up). NASDAQ held its edge at 20 (+2.6% vs +1.3%). Displacement momentum decays; the first two weeks are where this pattern pays.
The 50% rule is roughly a coin-flipOf ASX signals that touched the 50% zone, the stall saved capital 3,900 times and cut a winner 4,203 times. It's a momentum-quality filter, not a profit switch — its real job is routing slow setups out of the continuation playbook.
Out-of-sample is always weakerEvery market's last-12-months cohort underperforms its in-sample stats (ASX fwd-20: +1.5% IS vs +0.6% OOS). Expect live results closer to the OOS column than the headline.
Specs is a discovery lens, not an entry system5,296 replayed spec signals (5y ASX): buying the signal close returned −0.5% to −2.2% forward vs +1.8% for random entry on the same cheap universe, and A+ grades did no better. The JGH-type monsters are real but rare — the mean chase loses. Use SPECS ⚡ to find names waking up, then demand structure (a trap, a reclaim, a level) before anything else.

Survivorship health warningAll of the above is computed on stocks that still exist. The dead ones — which these signals would also have fired on — aren't in the data. Real-world numbers will be worse. Full reports live in the repo at phasemap/backtest/reports/.

These are measured properties of a mechanical ruleset on historical, survivor-biased data — not a promise about any future trade. Analysis only — not financial advice.
