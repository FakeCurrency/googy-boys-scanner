How the scanner works

VIVEK 5.0 is a three-lens system: three independent ways of reading the same market, each with its own scanner, its own rules and its own measured track record. When more than one lens fires on the same name in the same direction, that's the signal that sticks out like dog balls — and the system makes sure you see it. It doesn't predict prices or place trades.

The three lenses

  • VIVEK — the core lens. Price reacting at its 200-period SMA (Weekly, 3-Day or Daily/H4) — the line that separates trend from no-trend. Graded A+/A/B+ with a ready-made plan. Everything below this section is the VIVEK rulebook.
  • PHASEMAP — the trap lens. Finds where a move likely started: a liquidity sweep of obvious lows/highs followed by a displacement away. Zones, never single prices; states from TRAP SET to RUNNING. Its own tab, charts and nightly scan.
  • SPECS ⚡ — the discovery lens. Cheap, beaten-down names waking up on a 3× volume spike breaking out of a base. A shortlist generator, not an entry system — the backtest says demand structure before touching anything on it.

Multi-lens alignment — when 2+ lenses agree on one name, direction-aligned, a ⨂ banner appears on every page (🎯 for the rare full 3-lens house), Discord gets pinged, and the ALERTS page keeps the permanent log. Every lens's honest, replayed numbers — hit rates, expectancy, where the edge does and doesn't live — are on the INSIGHTS page, refreshed by a weekly backtest so they can't silently rot. Names you star anywhere collect on ★ MY NAMES.

The setup

Price trends away from the 200 SMA, then returns to test it. The level either holds (a long, trend resumes) or fails (a short, trend flips). It's read on two timeframes — Weekly (primary; slower, highest-conviction) and Daily / H4 (timing) — and only becomes ARMED once a trigger actually fires. Until then it's WATCHING: near the level, nothing yet.

The trigger — three entry types

Every armed setup is one of three reactions, labelled on the card and on every trade:

  • RECLAIM pierced the SMA, was rejected, then closed back on the trend side.
  • RETEST returned to the level and confirmed it with momentum.
  • BREAK broke minor structure sitting right at the level.

Filter by these under 200 SMA interaction.

Levels change with timeframe

  • Daily, 3-Day & Weekly each carry their own plan — level, entry, stop and targets — so Entry / SL / TP1–TP3 update when you switch timeframe on the chart. The 3-Day 200 SMA was added after it caught moves (XMR) the other two missed.
  • 4H shows real intraday candles but borrows the Daily plan's levels (no separate plan) — flagged with a notice on the chart.

Grades

A+ Cleanest, highest-conviction reaction. Tradeable
A Strong reaction, most signals present. Tradeable
B+ Partial setup, fewer signals. Watch list
WATCH Near the level / informational. Watching

Quality is scored on alignment, structure, volume, weekly confluence and momentum. An A+/A is demoted to B+ if it isn't armed or its R:R to TP2 is under 1.5 : 1 (flagged LOW R:R).

The trade plan

  • Entry — the trigger price on the selected timeframe.
  • SL — just beyond the reaction, so a true level failure invalidates it.
  • TP1 / TP2 / TP3 — prior structure first, measured R-multiples in clean air; headline R:R is to TP2.
  • Scale & trail — book partials at each TP (e.g. 25 / 50 / 15%); SL to break-even at TP1, then below new support at TP2, never against the trade.

Journal & track record

The autonomous paper bot takes A+ setups only — max 10 per market, one per symbol, short slots reserved, daily loss guards — and its book on the journal page is the system's one and only track record. Every trade keeps its entry-type label, timeframe and grade.
