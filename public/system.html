3 · The three lenses

VIVEK 5.0 — the core (SCAN tab)

Price reacting at its 200-SMA on Weekly / 3-Day / Daily levels. Three entry triggers, colour-coded by backtest edge: reclaim (close back above the 200-SMA after rejection — carries the edge) · break (break of small structure at the level — okay) · retest (retest + confirmation — weak; the bot skips it). Each setup carries per-timeframe plans (entry / SL / TP1-3), ARMED (trigger fired) vs WATCHING (near the level), grade hysteresis, and the still-forming bar is dropped so grades don’t wobble intraday.

PhaseMap — the trap lens

Liquidity behaviour, zone-based (zones are always bands, never single prices). States: TRAP_SET → SWEPT → RUNNING / DISPLACED → STALLED → COMPLETE/DEAD. Deterministic — no AI in the scan path; ruleset is versioned (RULESET_VERSION bumps on any parameter change). The owner’s master spec is law for its maths.

Specs — the discovery lens

Sub-$0.50 names breaking out of a base on a ≥3× volume spike. Its own backtest says it’s a shortlist generator, not an entry system — treat SPECS results as candidates to chart, not signals to buy.

🔗 Multi-lens confluence: when 2+ lenses agree on the same name in the same direction you get banners on the dashboard/lens pages, an ALERTS page entry, and (for triples) a Discord ping.

6 · Sizing & cost model (identical for bot, manual journal, cloud watcher)

  • Book: ONE pool of $150,000 across all three markets (was $10,000 per market → $30,000; changed 2026-07-28 with fixed sizing). Any market can hold any share of the 30 slots. The ASX side runs in A$; the journal converts its P&L to US$ at the scan’s published AUD/USD rate (data/fx.json) so combined totals never mix currencies at face value.
  • No compounding (deliberate): sizing always uses the starting book, never the running balance, so per-trade figures stay comparable for the whole forward test. Revisited when anything goes live.
  • Position size — fixed notional: $5,000 of stock bought per entry, every entry, capped by a $150,000 total-open ceiling (30 × $5,000 exactly). 1R = the dollars risked and $ P&L is still R × risk-$ — but under fixed sizing that dollar figure now varies with the stop distance rather than being the same slice of equity every time: a 2% stop risks $100, a 12% stop risks $600, bounded $50–$1,250 by the 1%/25% stop-width rules. Position count, not position size, is the risk dial. Set position_notional to 0 to revert to the risk-% path below, which is retained intact.
  • Risk per trade (only when fixed notional is off): 0.35% of the book, clamped 0.25–0.5%.
  • Leverage cap on notional: 5× stocks · 3× crypto — tight stops can’t create oversized positions.
  • Costs (baked into every realised R): commission ASX 2 · NASDAQ 1 · crypto 6 bps + slippage ASX 5 · NASDAQ 4 · crypto 8 bps. A stop/manual close pays slippage; a resting TP limit doesn’t.
  • Honest fills: a stop that gaps through fills at the worse price (never better than the stop); a target never credits overshoot.

9 · Safety gates

  • Paper only. No real orders anywhere. Live execution requires — all together — per-market MODE="live" + VIVEK_LIVE_CONFIRMED=True + a wired, reviewed broker layer. None exist today.
  • Daily-loss guard per market (above) + a separate kill-switch workflow for the legacy journal.
  • Schema gate: every scan verifies the published JSON carries the current schema version — code/data drift fails the workflow instead of silently hiding features.
  • Atomic writes (temp + rename) + corrupt-file quarantine on every journal/book load.
  • Config-first: every threshold lives in scanner/config.py / phasemap/config.py; bot constants republished to bot_rules.json each scan and drift-checked in the UI.

10 · Evidence — why these rules

A walk-forward backtest replays the real engine bar-by-bar (causal, pessimistic intrabar fills: stop checked before target). Headline findings that set today’s rules:

  • Long-only ex-NASDAQ ≈ +0.46R/trade, PF ≈ 2.0, ~56% win.
  • Weekly reclaim is the cleanest cell (~65% win, +0.85R, PF ≈ 3.3) → bot prefers 1W + reclaim.
  • Shorts ≈ −0.5R/trade everywhere → long-only. Retests ~flat → skipped. A ≈ breakeven → A+ only.

Forward evidence accrues in the journal’s Edge Tracker (expectancy per setup cell from real closed trades — needs ~20 per cell to mean anything) and refreshes automatically: weekly (canonical) + monthly (long-only).

11 · Known limitations (read before trusting any number)

  • ~15-min delayed data; the bot marks positions hourly at scans (manual trades: every 5 min via the watcher). Real gaps can and do blow through stops — losses beyond −1R are recorded honestly.
  • Survivorship bias in backtests (current index membership). Discount NASDAQ cells hardest.
  • Paper ≠ broker: the model prices commission + slippage but not CFD spread or overnight financing (~7–8%/yr on notional at CMC) — your real P&L will lag the paper book.
  • Cron is best-effort: scans can run minutes late; weekday crons don’t know market holidays.
  • Zero-to-few closed trades so far — the forward record, not the backtest, is the verdict. Judge on R over 40+ closed trades.
