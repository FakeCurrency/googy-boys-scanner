# Why /momentum shows four ASX names

Four rows next to a dense 5.0 deck looks broken. It is the filter working.

**ASX**: universe 2,047 → 1,165 downloaded → 298 scanned → 867 gated → **4 hits**.
**NASDAQ**: 1,426 → 1,425 → 1,157 → 268 gated → **20 hits**.

The scan runs **mode A with a 1-bar window**. Mode A selects on Rule A alone —
a regular RSI divergence off a strict both-sides pivot — and the 1-bar window
means the divergence must have become knowable on the **last closed bar**. Not
"has a divergence somewhere in the last month": confirmed on the most recent
bar, once. That is a handful of names a day by construction, and widening it is
a scan-rule change, not a display fix. **The cron is not being touched.**

The gates do most of the rest before any rule runs: on ASX the top three are
95 non-operating listings (funds, LICs, preferred lines), 27 names with no
volume in five bars, and 22 with too little history to seed a 200 EMA. Those
are refusals to score something that cannot be scored, not misses.

For contrast, the deck's A+ count answers a different question — a grade on
every name that set up, with no freshness window at all. The two numbers are
not comparable and the page should not try to make them look it.

## Coverage caveat

ASX downloaded only **1,165 of 2,047** with `reused: 0` — a cold frame cache on
first run, so ~43% of the universe returned nothing and was never scanned. The
four hits are drawn from 298 names, not 2,047. The next scheduled run should
warm the cache; if ASX coverage stays near half after a few runs that is a data
problem worth chasing separately. NASDAQ downloaded 1,425 of 1,426, so the
gap is ASX-specific rather than a scanner fault.
