## 1. The gate, from live code

The bot decides takeability in three places, and every predicate below is quoted from live code:

- **Direction.** `scanner/broker/vivek_bot.py:126`: `if not VIVEK_BOT_ALLOW_SHORTS and _direction(row) == "short"` skips the row (`shorts_disabled`). `VIVEK_BOT_ALLOW_SHORTS = False`.
- **Funds/REITs.** `:130`: `if VIVEK_BOT_EXCLUDE_FUNDS and _is_fund_or_reit(row)` skips it (`fund_reit`). This is the bot's keyword/sector test, not the scan's display-only `is_product` flag.
- **Data age.** `:136`: a row whose `data_age_days` exceeds `VIVEK_BOT_MAX_DATA_AGE_DAYS` (3) is skipped (`stale_data`).
- **Grade.** `:118`: `grade = row.get("grade_raw") or row.get("grade")`. This is the RAW grade; the smoothed one is only a fallback for payloads that predate `grade_raw`. `:143`: `if grade not in grades` skips it (`grade_excluded`), with `VIVEK_BOT_GRADES = ("A+", "A")`.
- **The cells.** `:83` `_pick_plan` walks `VIVEK_BOT_ENTRY_CELLS = {"1W": ("reclaim", "break"), "3D": ("reclaim",), "1D": ("break",)}` in that order. It takes the first plan that is `armed`, carries entry/stop/tp1-3, and whose `entry_trigger` the cell lists. Anything else is `no_cell_plan`.
- **R:R and level order.** `:155`: the levels must be ordered. `:160`: `if rr < min_rr` skips it, with `VIVEK_BOT_MIN_RR = 1.5` measured to TP2.
- **Stop cap.** `:169`: `abs(e - s) / e * 100 > VIVEK_BOT_MAX_STOP_PCT` (25) is `wide_stop`, where `e, s = plan["entry"], plan["stop"]`. That is the signal close, not the fill. `:180`: under `VIVEK_BOT_MIN_STOP_PCT` (1) is `stop_too_tight`.
- **Price and liquidity.** `plan_trade` (`:343`) adds the per-market minimum price, the ADV floor, and notional at or under 2% of ADV.
- **Book limits.** `decide()` (`:516`) adds one position per symbol, a 7-day re-entry cooldown after a stop, 30 open across all markets, $150,000 of notional, and 3 per sector per market.
- **The level gate.** `scanner/broker/vivek_run.py:1450` `_apply_level_gate` runs before `decide()`. `:617` keeps only rows whose `level_tf` is in `VIVEK_BOT_LEVEL_TF_ALLOW = ("weekly", "3d")`, and a missing `level_tf` is dropped.
- **Fill.** `:1470` fills in session only, with the loss guard clear, at the live quote (`:1473`), after the NASDAQ earnings buffer.
- **Management, not entry.** `VIVEK_BOT_MAX_HOLD_DAYS = 28` time-stops a position.

`public/data/bot_rules.json` (generated 2026-09-23T23:49:37Z) publishes the same grades, cells, 25/1 stop band, 1.5 R:R, long-only, funds excluded, 3 per sector, 30 open and 28 days. It does **not** publish the level gate: `VIVEK_BOT_LEVEL_TF_ALLOW` has no key there, so the rulebook the site reads is missing the one predicate that turns the four cells into the #42 sleeve.

The predicates, as the code reads today (`tests/test_bot_vs_cells.py` fails if this block and the code drift apart):

```text
direction        = long only
grades           = A+, A
grade_source     = grade_raw
cells            = 1W reclaim, 1W break, 3D reclaim, 1D break
cell_walk        = 1W > 3D > 1D
level_tf         = weekly, 3d
level_tf_missing = dropped
max_stop_pct     = 25
min_stop_pct     = 1
stop_basis       = plan entry, then fill
min_rr           = 1.5
exclude_funds    = True
max_per_sector   = 3
max_open_total   = 30
max_hold_days    = 28
```

**Gate vs sleeve, one sentence:** the gate is the four cells + weekly/3D level + stop <= 25%, but it reads the 25% off the signal close where #42 read it off the fill, so a fill that moves away from the close can hold a stop over 25%. That is the only way it is wider than the sleeve, and three open positions sit there today.

Everywhere else the gate is *narrower* than #42's sleeve:

- R:R >= 1.5 and the 1% minimum stop;
- the price and liquidity floors;
- one position per symbol, 3 per sector and 30 open.

It also takes one plan per symbol, walking 1W > 3D > 1D, where the replay held one slot per timeframe. And it *manages* differently: a 28-day time stop, plus the owner's hand closes, where the replay rode every runner to its stop or to the end of the data.

One label is wrong. `plan_trade` hard-codes `"grade": "A+"` on every ticket (`vivek_bot.py:406`). Since A became takeable on 2026-09-21, an A take is therefore recorded as A+.
