**Method.** 17 subsystem auditors covered every non-test source file, the 24 workflows, the Cloudflare Functions, the frontend and the committed data. Each finding then went to two adversarial verifiers. The first tried to make the shipped code fail by running it. The second checked CLAUDE.md, the docs and the code comments for a documented decision, and traced a real production caller. ## Themes (several bugs, one root cause)

1. **Unfinished bars leak in at session edges.** Your 2026-10-06 "ASX close is 16:40" ruling reached `scan_gate`, the digest and `ignition_asx_due`. It never reached `scan._bar_is_forming` (#2), `ignition/run.py` (#22), `momentum_due.py` (#66) or PhaseMap's equity forming-bar rule (#8). Momentum crypto (#85) and `history_archive` (#59) have the same shape.
2. **The TOP100 #13 window model was finished in only one place.** The guard's open leg measures from `day_marks`. The guard's closed leg (#1), the kill switch (#3), the guard's price input (#11) and the dollar basis of `risk_usd` (#12) all still mix whole-life or signal-price numbers into a daily figure.
3. **The heartbeat became the clock on 2026-10-06, but the code still treats it as an exception.** The must-change gate (#5) and the status sheet's amber "the cron missed" label (#81) both key off `schedule`.
4. **Crypto identity and venue protection does not reach every path.** Yahoo fallback freshness (#4), quantized history (#16), gap frames (#17), the edge ledgers (#30), the crypto backtests (#36) and held-position charts (#45) all bypass it.
5. **Partial exits.** The journal's "Now" price (#46) and `book_stress` (#31) both forget that `unreal_r` is already scaled by the remaining fraction.
6. **Market-switch races on the lens pages.** A late response lands on the wrong market in `phasemap.js` (#51), `specs.js` (#83), `momentum.js` (#84) and `app.js`'s detail sidecar (#41).

## Worth raising, not counted

## CRITICAL (1)

### 1. Loss guard's closed leg charges a stop-out's whole-life R to the exit day, so a crash day that stops out trailed winners reads as roughly flat and new entries carry on

- **Where:** `scanner/broker/vivek_guard.py:231`, `scanner/broker/vivek_guard.py:238`
- **Severity:** critical · **Fix class:** changes what the bot takes or when the guard trips - needs your OK
- **Found by:** bot-decision + bot-runner + live-data-contracts (independently, 3 areas) · reproduced by execution: yes

Since TOP100 #13 the guard measures each OPEN position from a per-window reference price (day_marks), and the module docstring says the windows 'telescope exactly'. The CLOSED leg was never converted. For a trade whose exit_date falls in the window it still charges total = realized_r x risk_usd, which is whole-life P&L measured from ENTRY, and only subtracts partial exits dated before the window (the #14 fix). The final exit leg is never measured from the window's reference, even though closed rows keep their day_marks (the live AZY closed row has them through its exit day). This has three effects. (1) A position that ran up and then hit its trailed stop shows its whole give-back as about 0 on the day it closes. (2) An old loser that finally stops out has its whole-life loss charged to that one day. (3) Summing the daily windows over a position's life no longer equals its total P&L.

**Failure scenario.** I ran vivek_run.run_market on NASDAQ (dry_run, scratch book) with 4 positions: entry 100, risk 10, risk_usd 625, TP1 already banked, stop trailed to breakeven 100, carried into the day at 130. Today's price is 101, so they stay open: the guard reads -5437.5 (real -5438) and breached=True. Today's price is 99, so the trailed stops fill: the real day is worse at -5812, but the guard reads session_usd -210.0 with breached=False, and the fill step (`if is_open and not guard['breached']`) keeps opening new positions. The reverse also happens.

**Fix sketch.** In _window_pnl's closed loop, compute the in-window part with the same _window_r machinery as the open leg: ref = ref_price(t, since) using the closed row's retained day_marks; sum pct x (exit_px - ref)/risk for exits dated in [since, day]; subtract the in-window share of costs; multiply by risk_usd. Add a telescoping test that spans a close.


## HIGH (9)

### 2. Forming-bar drop ends at the raw 16:00 bell, but bot entries run to 16:15, so the bot opens trades on triggers from today's unfinished bar

- **Where:** `scanner/scan.py:177`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** changes what the bot takes or when the guard trips - needs your OK
- **Found by:** vivek-engine · reproduced by execution: yes

_bar_is_forming() says today's daily bar is complete as soon as the local clock passes VIVEK_JOURNAL_SESSION's raw close (16:00). It ignores VIVEK_JOURNAL_FEED_DELAY_MIN (15 min). The config comment says that delay is 'added to both ends at runtime', and the bot's entry gate (vivek_journal.market_open) does add it, so new entries stay allowed until 16:15. For every scan between 16:00 and 16:15 local, scan_vivek_market therefore keeps today's still-forming bar. That bar is 15 minutes delayed, and on the ASX it is also pre-auction: the repo's own MORNING_PLAYS_SLOT_GATE['asx'] treats the ASX bar as final only at 16:40. evaluate(), build_plans() and detect_trigger() then grade and arm on that bar, and vivek_run.run_market can open positions in the same run.

**Failure scenario.** NASDAQ: the cron-job.org hourly :10 ping fires at 16:10 New York every weekday, and the scan lands at about 16:10-16:15 (funnel_history shows 10-06 16:14 and 10-07 16:10). _bar_is_forming returns False, so today's bar, which reflects about 15:55 prices on the delayed feed, is graded as complete. A 1D break, 3D reclaim or 1W reclaim can arm on it, and market_open() is still True, so decide() opens a paper position. If the final close (or the ASX auction print at about 16:10, which Yahoo shows at about 16:30) undoes the trigger, the position stays open anyway. This has happened in the live book: entries opened at ASX AFG 07-20 16:08 and JHX 09-07 16:14, and NASDAQ NXST and PRGS 08-31 16:01.

**Fix sketch.** In _bar_is_forming, treat the bar as forming until close + VIVEK_JOURNAL_FEED_DELAY_MIN. For the ASX, use the MORNING_PLAYS_SLOT_GATE['asx'] finality time (16:40). Simplest: read a per-market 'bar final' minute from one config map (ALERT_RETURNS_BAR_FINAL already exists: ASX 16:45, NASDAQ 16:30) and use it here. That way no trigger arms on an unfinished bar while market_open() still allows entries.

### 3. Kill switch checks WHOLE-LIFE book P&L against the DAILY loss limit (the TOP100 #13 bug, never fixed here)

- **Where:** `scanner/broker/kill_switch.py:301`, `scanner/broker/kill_switch.py:309`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** changes what the bot takes or when the guard trips - needs your OK
- **Found by:** bot-decision + live-data-contracts (independently, 2 areas) · reproduced by execution: yes

run_standalone, the half-hourly 'daily-loss kill-switch' that CLAUDE.md calls the live loss guard on the paper book, builds each market's 'session P&L' in _book_market_journal. For each open position it uses _unreal_r(p, quote) x risk_usd, which is P&L measured from ENTRY, plus the whole-life banked realized_r x risk_usd. For each row closed today it uses whole-life realized_r with no subtraction of earlier partial exits. It then compares that total with the DAILY limit (VIVEK_BOT_MAX_DAILY_LOSS_PCT x equity = $4,500). This is exactly the defect vivek_guard fixed on 2026-07-28. A market holding old winners can lose more than the daily limit today and the switch stays silent. A market holding old losers that are flat today fires on every :15/:45 run.

**Failure scenario.** I ran run_standalone(dry_run=True) on a scratch book, with _live_marks monkeypatched to fixed quotes and alerts stubbed. (a) 10 NASDAQ positions up +2R since September that each lose 1.6R today: vivek_guard's real session P&L is -$4,800 (breached), but the kill switch returns triggered=[]. (b) 10 NASDAQ positions down -0.75R since September and flat today: vivek_guard reads $0 (not breached), but the kill switch logs 'KILL SWITCH TRIGGERED - bot book [nasdaq] P&L = $-5625.00' and returns triggered=['nasdaq']. With ALPACA keys exported (kill_switch.yml passes them), each such run calls Alpaca close_all_positions + cancel_all_orders, and the ::error:: annotation fires every half hour.

**Fix sketch.** Have _book_market_journal reuse vivek_guard's window maths: call vivek_guard.session_pnl(book, market, market_day, price_of) with a price_of built from the live quotes, falling back to last_mark, and compare session_usd (and optionally the weekly figure) with the limits. Do not hand-roll whole-life unrealised plus banked R.

### 4. Yahoo fallback leg of data.fetch has no bar-age gate: a frozen Yahoo crypto series is accepted as fresh and freezes a held position's mark (stop never tested, never counted unpriced)

- **Where:** `scanner/data.py:476`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** can only remove a price/name (fail-closed) - fix shipped before under that rule
- **Found by:** data-layer · reproduced by execution: yes

EXCHANGE_MAX_BAR_AGE_DAYS (1 day) is enforced only inside exchange_data.download_klines' venue loop. The Yahoo fallback in fetch() checks identity only, never bar age. Every coin whose exchange pair is stale-rejected (BEAM, BTT, LIT, XMR on every current run) or that no exchange lists (~60 coins: BDX, WBT, OKB, KCS, HTX...; the bot has held BDX/WBT/OKB) is priced through this unguarded leg. A Yahoo frame whose newest bar is days old is stamped source=yahoo/identity=ref|anchor and handed on as a FRESH frame. mark_age_h returns 0.0 for anything not cache-reused, so vivek_run.price_of treats the frozen last close as a live mark. The position is marked, unpriced_runs is reset, the stop is tested against the frozen price and the guard values it at that price.

**Failure scenario.** A held crypto position (stop 0.90) whose coin has no live exchange pair; Yahoo's daily series for it stops printing (last bar 6 days ago, close 1.00) while the real price (CoinGecko 0.75) falls through the stop. scan/run.py fetch -> identity 'ref' passes (1.00/0.75 = 1.33 <= 1.40) -> merge_with_cache -> vivek_run.run_market: position stays OPEN at last_mark 1.0 with no unpriced_runs, and the anchor is re-stamped to a date inside the frozen series. kill_switch._live_marks -> fetch(held_price_kwargs) prices it at 1.0 via 'anchor'. The stop never fires until the real price drifts more than 40% from the frozen one; after that the position is just 'unpriced'.

**Fix sketch.** In fetch()'s Yahoo fallback loop, apply the same gate: if exchange_data.bar_age_days(f) > EXCHANGE_MAX_BAR_AGE_DAYS, then stale.setdefault(base[t], []).append('yahoo') and del fb[t]. This makes the coin refused, and merge_with_cache already blocks a cached 'yahoo' frame for it. Optionally also make price_of treat a crypto frame whose last bar is older than 1 UTC day as unpriced.

### 5. Must-change gate disarmed for heartbeat-dispatched scans (~95% of stock scans): a market's bot book can freeze with every run green

- **Where:** `.github/workflows/scan.yml:520`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** no trade change - safe to fix
- **Found by:** publish-alerts + workflows (independently, 2 areas) · reproduced by execution: yes

The per-market must-change asserts in scan.yml's commit step (TOP100 #49: `scan output [$m]` and `bot book [$m]`) only run when GITHUB_EVENT_NAME is `schedule`. Every other event falls through to an advisory check on the COMBINED book (`|| echo ::warning::`). The heartbeat healer (functions/api/heartbeat.js:236) dispatches scan.yml as `workflow_dispatch` with inputs {market, reason:"heartbeat"}. Since 2026-10-06 cron-job.org pinging the heartbeat is "THE REAL CLOCK", so nearly every stock scan takes the advisory branch. The committed funnel_history.json `trigger` column for rows since 2026-10-01: ASX 72 heartbeat vs 4 cron, NASDAQ 64 vs 6, and crypto via scan.yml 97 heartbeat. The per-market asserts were written for exactly one failure: run.py:394-395 swallows any bot-layer exception into `print("bot book: skipped")` and the process exits 0.

**Failure scenario.** A new row field or a bad frame makes vivek_run.run_market raise for ASX on every scan (e.g. a KeyError). On each cron-job.org heartbeat: run.py prints 'bot book: skipped' and exits 0. asx_vivek.json and asx_prices.json are republished, so the heartbeat sees ASX fresh and dispatches nothing more. `vivek_run --verify` passes because the unchanged book is consistent. The commit step takes the workflow_dispatch branch and goes green. The watchdog sees crypto's fresh book. Result: ASX positions are never marked or stop/time-stop tested, and no new ASX entries are taken, for as long as the bug persists. Only a rare on-time GitHub cron would fail red.

**Fix sketch.** In the commit step, treat `github.event.inputs.reason == 'heartbeat'` like `schedule`, e.g. compute a GATED=1 env from event_name/reason and test that instead of GITHUB_EVENT_NAME. Optionally have watchdog.probe_content raise a per-market finding when a market's canonical book lags its own `<m>_prices.json` generated_at by more than N hours.

### 6. close_position redispatch can never re-queue an evicted close: gh runs with no checkout and no --repo

- **Where:** `.github/workflows/close_position.yml:419`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** no trade change - safe to fix
- **Found by:** workflows · reproduced by execution: yes

The redispatch job (the owner-decided recovery for a close evicted from the scan mutex, or one that lost five push races) has no actions/checkout step and sets neither GH_REPO nor --repo. gh resolves the base repo from the git remotes of the current directory and does not read GITHUB_REPOSITORY. In the empty workspace, every call fails with `failed to run git: fatal: not a git repository`: `gh run view`, both `gh run list`, and `gh workflow run close_position.yml`. The first three are swallowed by `|| echo 0`, so it reads as 'eviction, nothing pending'. The final `gh workflow run` then aborts the step under bash -e, so no attempt 2 is ever dispatched. dispatch_scan.yml shows the working form (`--repo "$GITHUB_REPOSITORY"`).

**Failure scenario.** The owner taps close-all or closes from the stalled strip while an ASX scan holds the `scan` mutex. /api/close replies 'close queued'. The close job sits pending and is evicted by the next arrival (crypto heartbeat :22, crypto_bot :22/:52, ASX hourly :10), giving needs.close.result == 'cancelled'. Redispatch prints 'Evicted from the scan mutex - re-dispatching as attempt 2.' and then dies on the git error. The position stays OPEN on the only track record; the only signal is a red 'Close position (manual)' run. The push_exhausted path (TOP100 #47) is equally dead.

**Fix sketch.** Add `GH_REPO: ${{ github.repository }}` to the step env (or pass --repo "$GITHUB_REPOSITORY" on all five gh calls). Make the test assert that the repo is resolvable, e.g. GH_REPO is present in the env.

### 7. Momentum charts: the 'My size' position sizer is never recomputed on a timeframe switch, so it shows the Daily box's unit count (unlabelled) while the 4H/3D/W box is on screen

- **Where:** `public/js/chart.js:2989`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** no trade change - safe to fix
- **Found by:** frontend-chart · reproduced by execution: yes

TOP100 #39 fixed the real-money sizer going stale on a TF switch by having applyVivekLevels call d._recalcMySize() after it swaps the plan (chart.js:3087). applyMomentumPlan, added later for the Momentum mode, updates d._activeLevels/d._activeTf but never calls the recompute hook. render() wires the sizer BEFORE the first applyTF, so on first paint the output line is computed from d.entry/d.stop (the Daily box) with no TF label, and every later TF switch leaves that number in place. **Fix sketch.** At the end of applyMomentumPlan (both the !pl and pl branches) call `if (typeof d._recalcMySize === "function") d._recalcMySize();`, and update the #ct-dir badge from pl.dir per timeframe.

### 8. PhaseMap scans NASDAQ on the still-forming session bar whenever the 08:30 UTC nightly runs late (it ran at 11:52 EDT on 2026-10-07)

- **Where:** `phasemap/engine/scanner.py:28`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** PhaseMap detection/output - spec is law, needs your OK (+ RULESET_VERSION bump)
- **Found by:** phasemap-specs · reproduced by execution: yes

drop_forming_bar only drops the in-progress bar for CONFIG.drop_forming_bar_markets = ("crypto",). Equity markets are trusted on the claim in config.py:91-95 that they "are scanned post-close by the nightly schedule, so their last bar is already complete". That only holds if the 08:30 UTC cron fires on time. GitHub's scheduler often runs it hours late (REFINEMENTS.md:80 records 12:58 and 17:01 UTC runs). A delay of 5h to 11h30 (EDT) or 6h to 12h30 (EST) puts the run inside the NASDAQ session. yfinance's daily download then includes today's partial OHLC row, so sweeps, displacements and DEAD kills get decided on a candle that keeps changing until 16:00 NY. This is the same H3 problem that ruleset 1.3.0 fixed for crypto, and the Melbourne run_date labels the snapshot a day ahead.

**Failure scenario.** On 2026-10-07 the nightly committed at 15:52:17 UTC (commit 3572a2e, 11:52 EDT, about 2.5h into the NASDAQ session). 750 NASDAQ chart files end on a 2026-10-07 candle, while crypto's correctly end on 10-06. 95 published NASDAQ records hinge on that partial bar: 67 SWEPT, 5 DISPLACED and 23 DEAD. confluence.yml fired from workflow_run and logged 16 NASDAQ PHASEMAP+VIVEK alignments into the permanent ALERTS log at 15:52:58 UTC (ZVRA, NCNO, LKFN, IPGP, FULT and AGNCP all swept on the 10-07 bar). alert_returns.py then reads those into the forward-return edge ledger. The snapshot is labelled run_date 2026-10-08.

**Fix sketch.** Drop the newest equity bar when the run instant is before that market's close on the bar's date (reuse config.MARKET_SCAN_WINDOWS / MORNING_PLAYS_SLOT_GATE closes). Alternatively gate the job like scan_gate. Because this is a detection-input change, bump RULESET_VERSION with the owner's sign-off.

### 9. Yahoo outage: YFinanceProvider re-downloads the whole universe once per ticker, then publishes an empty snapshot green (or times out the whole nightly)

- **Where:** `phasemap/data/provider.py:75`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** no trade change - safe to fix
- **Found by:** phasemap-specs · reproduced by execution: yes

get_daily_bars calls fetch_all() again whenever self._cache is empty. If every chunk of a market's download comes back empty (a throttle or outage), run_market's loop over provider.universe() triggers a full re-download for every ticker: N_tickers x ceil(N/75) yf.download calls. If the calls fail fast, run_market goes on to build and write_snapshot an empty results list over yesterday's latest.json, prunes every chart file (charted is empty) and returns normally, so main() exits 0. validate_published accepts an empty list and the must-change gate passes because run_date changed, so the wipe is committed green. If the calls are slow, the storm runs past the 120-min job timeout and kills the job before the commit step. That throws away the same night's already-computed NASDAQ and crypto snapshots and skips Specs.

**Failure scenario.** An ASX night where Yahoo returns nothing means 1,923 tickers x 26 chunks, about 50,000 yf.download calls, so the job hits timeout-minutes 120 and nothing is committed for any market. A crypto night (201 coins) means 603 failing calls, then crypto/latest.json is replaced by 0 results and all crypto chart files are deleted, with a green run.

**Fix sketch.** Use a fetched flag instead of `if not self._cache` so fetch_all runs once. In run_market, refuse to write or prune, and exit non-zero, when coverage is 0 or collapses against the previous latest.json.

### 10. Candidate pre-filter hides weekly-level setups the engine would find, so every replay drops trades on the top-ranked level

- **Where:** `scanner/vivek_backtest.py:91`
- **Severity:** high · **Fix class:** no trade change - safe to fix
- **Found by:** backtests · reproduced by execution: yes

The docstring of `_candidate_mask` says it is a superset of the engine's in-play test, so it only saves work. That is not true for the weekly level. The mask computes `wk = close.resample("W-FRI").last()` and then `wk.rolling(config.VIVEK_SMA).mean()`. It does not call dropna and it has no min_periods. The engine (`vivek._weekly_sma200`) does `.dropna()` and uses `window = min(VIVEK_SMA, n)` once n >= VIVEK_MIN_WEEKLY_BARS (60). This causes two divergences. (1) Between week 60 and week 200 of a slice, the engine scores weekly-level setups on an n-week average, but the mask's weekly SMA is NaN, so `near_w` is False. (2) One empty W-FRI bucket (for example a 1-2 week halt; `data.download` dropna's those rows) leaves NaN in the window, and the mask's weekly SMA stays NaN for the next 200 weeks. The engine skips the gap.

**Failure scenario.** An ASX small cap is suspended for 2 weeks in 2018. On a 10y long-only run (vivek_backtest.yml), every weekly-200 reaction until about 2022 that is not also within 5.2% of the daily or 3D 200 is never evaluated, so those A/A+ weekly-level trades are missing from the report. On the 5y weekly run (lens_backtest.yml), weekly-proxy setups in the first ~200 weeks of each name are filtered out the same way. This includes young listings and most crypto coins, where the live scan itself uses the proxy window.

**Fix sketch.** Build the mask's weekly SMA the way the engine does: `wk = close.resample('W-FRI').last().dropna(); wsma = wk.rolling(config.VIVEK_SMA, min_periods=config.VIVEK_MIN_WEEKLY_BARS).mean().reindex(df.index, method='ffill')`. Add a test that replay_symbol with the shipped mask produces the same trades as with an all-True mask on a frame with a gap and on a frame with fewer than 200 weeks.


## MEDIUM (45)

### 11. Runner's loss guard is fed the raw price that _mark_sanity just rejected (split or bad print), faking or masking a breach

- **Where:** `scanner/broker/vivek_run.py:1379`
- **Severity:** medium · **Fix class:** changes what the bot takes or when the guard trips - needs your OK
- **Found by:** bot-decision + bot-runner (independently, 2 areas) · reproduced by execution: yes

In run_market every held position's price goes through _mark_sanity. A move beyond VIVEK_MARK_SANITY_PCT (35% for stocks, 60% for crypto) is treated as a split or bad print and the position is frozen. The _mark_sanity docstring says it freezes 'so unreal_r/unreal_usd are not stamped from a bad print, and the loss guard reads no lie'. But vivek_guard.check is then called with the unfiltered price_of, which returns the frame's raw last close, so the guard values the frozen position at exactly the price that was rejected.
