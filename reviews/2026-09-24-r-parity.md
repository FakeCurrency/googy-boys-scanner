# Like-for-like R across the four lenses -- 2026-09-24

One fill rule, one cost table, one notional, one minimum stop, applied to every lens's own signals over the same downloaded bars. The HEADLINE IS LONG ONLY; shorts are in the appendix and are in no win% above it.

- **House fill** (the headline): a stop is a resting order, filled at the stop or at the open of a bar that gapped through it.
- **Worst print** (second column): the SAME trades, a stop filled at the bar's worst print -- the 5.0 backtest's convention. Exit timing is identical, so both columns are one trade set; the difference is purely the fill.
- Costs: the house table, basis points per side -- ASX slippage 5 + commission 2; NASDAQ slippage 4 + commission 1; CRYPTO slippage 8 + commission 6. $ at $1,000 a trade = R x 1,000 x risk / entry. Minimum stop 1% of entry: a closer stop is not a trade in any lens.
- $ weights each trade by its stop width (a 20% stop at +1R makes $200, a 5% stop $50), so a lens or market with wide stops can show $ and R with different signs; R/trade is the like-for-like number.
- Window: 5y of daily bars; trades entered 2021-11-10 to 2026-09-23. Universe: today's, less the bot's fund/REIT exclusion, identically for all four lenses.

## What the paper bot should keep taking, and what it should not

Keep taking the four high-conviction cells, long only, at A/A+ on a weekly or 3-day level. That is exactly what the bot does now. As the bot takes them (stop at or under 25%), they made +0.263R a trade on the house fill and still +0.179R on the 5.0 worst print (n=2,594), so their edge does not depend on which fill you believe. The 1W reclaim and 3D reclaim cells carry most of it (+0.308/+0.262 and +0.271/+0.201). The 1D break cell is the thinnest (+0.156/+0.077 on 568 trades): watch it, don't cut it yet. NASDAQ is the core (+0.270/+0.196 as taken). The ASX leg is thinner (+0.178/+0.074) and is where a harsh fill nearly erases it. The 28 crypto trades are too few to read either way. What it should not take: every other A/A+ 5.0 long makes +0.097R on the house fill and -0.024R at the worst print, and plain 1D is the same (+0.093/-0.034), so their edge exists only under the kinder fill. Do not widen the bot past the cells. No shorts from any lens: every short book here is negative under both fills. Do not add PhaseMap (-0.024/-0.103, and -0.021 on its own close-only exits) or Specs (-0.072/-0.136, a shortlist, as its own backtest said). Do not add Momentum either. Its +0.114R needs a resting stop filled at the level, and at the worst print it turns to -0.097R. Its median stop is the tightest here (7.6% against 5.0's 16.2%), so every tick of fill slippage is a bigger slice of R. All of this is on today's universe, so the survivorship flattery above applies to every number in it.
