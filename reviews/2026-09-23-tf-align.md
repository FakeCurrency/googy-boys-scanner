## What was wrong, measured

1. **4H drifted under daylight saving.** UTC buckets equal 10:00 / 14:00 Sydney
   only while Sydney is UTC+10. Under AEDT, 192 of the 1086 UTC 4H bars opened
   at 07:00 Sydney. After the change, every ASX 4H bar opens at 10:00 or 14:00
   Sydney: 23:00Z / 03:00Z under AEDT and 00:00Z / 04:00Z under AEST, across
   both clock changes.
2. **The closing auction was inside the last 4H bar. This was the 5c.**
   Yahoo's hourly ASX feed ends each day with a 16:00 bar that is the closing
   auction alone (open = high = low = close). On 13 Aug that bar printed 6.49,
   while the 14:00 and 15:00 hours both closed 6.46. The evidence says
   TradingView's intraday ASX session ends at 16:00 and leaves the auction out:
   excluding it reproduces TV's entry to the cent. That is inferred from the
   match, not read from TradingView's documentation. Its daily bar still closes
   on the auction, which is why Daily already matched. With the 16:00 bar
   included, the 14:00 4H bar closed 6.49, and the EMAs put the last cross one
   bar earlier, at the 10:00 bar's 6.51. With it excluded, the cross lands on
   the 14:00 bar and closes at 6.46, which is TradingView's entry.
   This is a session definition, not an adjustment to any price: the cash
   session is 10:00–16:00, and the 16:00 print is outside it.
3. **3D padded weekends.** Bucketing by three calendar days left 319 of the
   1108 "3-day" bars holding a single session (333 held two, 456 three). The
   3D Auto box shown before (Entry 4.72) came from those padded bars.
4. **Daily bars carry a UTC session-open stamp.** 1134 of the 2353 daily bars
   are stamped 23:00Z on the previous UTC day, which is 10:00 AEDT. Anything
   that groups daily bars by date has to use the exchange's own date.
   `sessionWeeks` does; the existing Sat→Fri weeks were already immune to this.

NASDAQ is handled with the same rule (America/New_York, 09:30–16:00). The AAPL
hourly fixture runs 09:30…15:30 plus one 16:00 closing print. 4H bars open at
09:30 and 13:30 New York, and the 16:00 print is dropped. Crypto has no session
(24/7) and keeps the UTC buckets.

## Not verified against TradingView

- **3D anchor.** Bars are three sessions counted from the first loaded session.
  TradingView's multi-day anchor is not documented here and I could not read
  it. It makes no difference to ELS today: all three possible anchors put the
  last cross ~118 bars back, so there is no Auto box on any of them. A TV 3D
  timestamp would settle it.
- **W and 3D Auto boxes.** Both are empty on ELS, matching the template's
  60-bar freshness rule. There is no TV number to compare against.
- **4H stop, $0.006 short of 7.39.** The stop is the 5-bar swing plus
  0.25 × ATR(14). I did not investigate the 0.6c residual. Small differences
  between Yahoo's hourly feed and TradingView's could produce it, but that is
  unconfirmed. It is inside the $0.02 acceptance.
- **The 4H box goes stale soon.** It is 56 bars old on the fixture, so it
  falls outside the 60-bar window within about two sessions. TradingView
  applies the same rule. The frozen fixture keeps the test stable.
